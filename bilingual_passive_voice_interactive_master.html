1. 受動態の基本原理（能動態との違い） 1. Core Passive Voice Mechanics

能動態 (Active Voice)
S (Yu Darvish) V (threw) O (the ball)
受動態 (Passive Voice)
O (The ball) be + Vpp (was thrown) by + S (by Yu Darvish)
なぜ受動態を使うのか？ Why use the Passive Voice?

動作を行った「主語(S)」よりも、動作を受けた「目的語(O)」に視点・重点を置きたい場合や、動作主が不明・一般人・明らかな場合に用いられます。

Used when focusing on the receiver (Object) rather than the actor (Subject), or when the actor is unknown, obvious, or general.

  • by + 動作主: 能動態の主語は `by + 目的格` に変化（I → by me, She → by her）Active Subject becomes `by + objective form` (I → by me, She → by her).
  • by の省略: 動作主が不明・一般的・当たり前の場合は `by ...` を省略。`by ...` is omitted if the actor is unknown, obvious, or general.

2. 第4文型（SVOO）の受動態：2つの受動態パターン 2. SVOO Pattern Passive: Two Transformations

重要 (Crucial)

SVOO（主語＋動詞＋人[O1]＋物[O2]）には目的語が2つあるため、「人」を主語にするパターンと「物」を主語にするパターンの2種類が存在します。 Because SVOO (Subject + Verb + Person[O1] + Thing[O2]) has two objects, it can form two distinct passive sentences depending on whether "Person" or "Thing" becomes the Subject.

Active Voice (能動態):
Brad gave Angela the ring.
Pattern A: 「人 (Angela)」が主語
Angela was given the ring (by Brad).
人 ＋ be動詞 ＋ 過去分詞 ＋ 物 ＋ by...
Pattern B: 「物 (The ring)」が主語
The ring was given to Angela (by Brad).
物 ＋ be動詞 ＋ 過去分詞 ＋ to/for ＋ 人 ＋ by...

「物」を主語にする時の前置詞の選択規則 (to vs. for) Preposition Choice for "Thing-as-Subject" (to vs. for)

to を使う動詞 相手への「到達・移動」が必須の動詞

相手（受け手）が存在しないと動作が成立しない動詞。 Verbs requiring a recipient for the action to be completed.

give (あげる) show (見せる) tell (伝える) teach (教える) send (送る) lend (貸す)
for を使う動詞 相手のために「準備・料理・調達」する動詞

相手がいなくても一人で行える動作（※「人」を主語にする受動態は原則不可）。 Actions done for someone's benefit. Usually CANNOT make the "person" the passive subject!

buy (買う) make (作る) cook (料理する) find (見つける)
注意 (Important Note): `buy`, `make`, `cook` などの `for` 型動詞は、「人」を主語にした受動態を作ることができません。 (✕ I was bought a cake. → 〇 A cake was bought for me.) For verbs like `buy`, `make`, and `cook`, you cannot make the Person the subject of a passive sentence!

3. 第5文型（SVOC）の受動態：補語(C)の残し方 3. SVOC Pattern Passive: Preserving Complement (C)

SVOC（主語＋動詞＋目的語＋補語）では、目的語(O)が受動態の主語になります。補語(C)は形を変えずに動詞の直後にそのまま残ります（S' + be + Vpp + C + by S）。 In SVOC (Subject + Verb + Object + Complement), Object (O) becomes the passive Subject. The Complement (C) remains directly after the verb unchanged.

Active (能動態)
They call the girl Angela. (SVOC)
Passive (受動態)
The girl is called Angela.
Active (能動態)
My father named me Yuka.
Passive (受動態)
I was named Yuka (by my father).
Active (能動態)
Mike left the door open.
Passive (受動態)
The door was left open (by Mike).

群動詞の受動態 (Phrasal Verbs) Passive with Phrasal Verbs

動詞＋前置詞/副詞のセットは「1つの動詞」として扱い、前置詞を落とさずに残します。 Treat verb + preposition/adverb combinations as a single verb unit. Do not drop the preposition!

talk to: I was talked to by two young women.
take care of: Our cats are taken care of by Mrs. Smith.
look up to: Mr. Green is looked up to by a lot of students.
bring up: He was brought up by a Chinese couple.

by 以外の前置詞を使う重要受動表現 Important Idiomatic Passives (No "by")

be known to (~に知られている)
be covered with (~でおおわれている)
be filled with (~で満たされている)
be injured in (~でけがをする)
be interested in (~に興味がある)
be surprised at (~に驚く)
be pleased with (~に喜んでいる)
be worried about (~について心配する)
